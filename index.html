About me

Developer, designer & storyteller.

I'm an Information Technology student at Cebu Eastern College. Most of my experience comes from class projects and our capstone, where I work with a team to plan, design, document, and build a web-based system for a real barangay.

I'm most interested in web development, UI design, and systems that make public services easier to use — and outside of code, I enjoy filmmaking. I co-directed and edited the short film music video Tahanan.

Right now I'm comfortable building responsive pages with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, and I'm still growing in back-end development with Django, databases, and deployment.
